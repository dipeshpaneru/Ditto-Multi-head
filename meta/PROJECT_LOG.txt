SELECTED PAPER AND EXPERIMENT
-----------------------------
Chen et al. (2023), Ditto: A Simple and Efficient Approach to Improve
Sentence Embeddings, EMNLP 2023, pp. 5868–5875.
Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.359/
Source: https://github.com/alibaba-damo-academy/SpokenNLP/tree/30fc9df96ee4023868107c02ed681346dac4fa5d/ditto

Research question: Can pretrained self-attention weights improve sentence
embeddings for semantic textual similarity?
Method: Ditto uses a selected head's diagonal self-attention values to
weight token representations. The comparison uses pretrained uncased BERT
and first–last pooling, with no model training. The paper's BERT layer 1,
head 10 maps to --layer 0 --head 9 in the evaluator.
